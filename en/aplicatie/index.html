Tools

The live clock shows the current time in Human precessional time on 36-hour and 18-hour dials and on the Earth clock, with the Sun and Moon at their real positions. If you choose the date and time of a solar eclipse, the Moon's shadow appears on the Earth clock, computed realistically (for example 12 August 2026, 17:46 UTC). The date tab shows the year, the eras and the next events. The calendar can be printed for any year, and the converter turns a date and time from standard time into the system and back.

A personal, dogma-independent way of measuring time: the hour as an angle, the year anchored to the solstice, eras from precession. It does not replace civil time or official standards.
